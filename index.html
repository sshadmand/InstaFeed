LESS BACK-AND-FORTH

From “something feels off”
to something you can act on.

Give testers a simple way to show you what they mean, without leaving the page.

  1. 01

    A tap captures the moment.

    The floating button splits into thumbs up and down on hover or tap. Choose a reaction to capture the page and record the URL, title, and browser details.

  2. 02

    A note adds the context.

    Testers can write a comment or dictate one with voice input, attached to the same screenshot and reaction.

  3. 03

    Your team sees the full picture.

    Organize feedback into private company, team, and project workspaces. Your team reviews screenshots and comments together in a protected inbox.

02 / ADD THE WIDGET

Connect your app in a few lines.

Load the hosted browser SDK and use your project’s public key. For a self-hosted deployment, replace the URL with your own domain.

Your project key is public by design; restrict allowed origins in project settings.

JAVASCRIPT / TYPESCRIPT
import { createFeedbackFab } from 'https://instafeed.chat/sdk/instafeed.es.js';

const widget = createFeedbackFab({
  endpoint: 'https://instafeed.chat',
  projectKey: 'ifp_YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_KEY',
});

03 / SELF-HOST IF YOU PREFER

Run the same platform on your own servers.

Deploy with Node.js and Postgres, then manage your own company workspaces and feedback data. The project includes the API, workspace, SDK, and migrations.

See the self-hosting guide for setup details.

Made to fit into your app.

The widget isolates its styles with Shadow DOM. Choose a specific element to capture, handle submission callbacks, or remove the widget when a component unmounts.

Know what gets captured.

Reports include the screenshot, reaction, page URL, title, browser details, and optional comment. Cross-origin iframes and protected media may not appear.
